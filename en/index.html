Research

Mass spectrometry & the origin of life

How do amino acids, lipids and other life-related molecules fragment under different ionisation and collision conditions? I match these lab results to Cassini’s mass spectra of the Enceladus plume, to build an interpretive framework for spotting biosignatures in future icy-moon missions.

Chemiluminescence & photopolymerisation

Using the light a chemical reaction emits to drive polymerisation: chemiluminescence is absorbed by a sulfonium-salt photoinitiator, which releases an acid that starts cationic polymerisation. No external light source is needed, and the result can be linear polymers or self-luminous crosslinked networks.
