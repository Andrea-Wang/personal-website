Research

Mass spectrometry, photochemistry and biosignature detection. PhD in the group of Prof. Bernd Abel at Leipzig University.

orcid.org/0009-0006-3904-917X

Current work

  • Impact-induced fragmentation and fragment-spectrum analysis with high-resolution Orbitrap MS
  • Hypervelocity-impact time-of-flight mass spectrometry (TOF-MS) experiments
  • Biosignature analysis for Enceladus (Cassini–Huygens)
  • Design and assembly of SELINA, an impact-ionisation platform for particle experiments

Interests

Mass spectrometry & instrumentation

High-resolution Orbitrap MS; collision-induced dissociation mechanisms; impact TOF-MS experiments and data analysis; design and assembly of the SELINA impact-ionisation platform; fragment-spectrum identification of biomolecules; biomarker prediction and detection for ocean worlds.

Microfluidics & fabrication

Microfluidic chip design and fabrication; DLP and FDM 3D printing; chemiluminescent microfluidic systems.

Data analysis & computational chemistry

Automated MS/MS data processing and visualisation in Python; quantum-chemical calculations with ORCA; bond dissociation energies and molecular fragmentation.

Materials & photochemistry

Polymer chemistry; photocuring and photopolymerisation; chemiluminescence-induced photopolymerisation; two-photon lithography; electroless metal patterning.
